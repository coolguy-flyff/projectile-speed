The model: everything in world units per client frame at 60 fps (×60 for units/sec). Base run speed 0.116 · jump = instant +0.2 vertical velocity · gravity −0.005 per frame · terminal fall 0.5. Scroll of Velocity: +30 speed points, jump height 175%. Scroll of Jumping: 150%. Projectile speeds are hardcoded in the client, and projectiles despawn after 10 s — outliving the bomb counts as escaping. It re-aims at your position every frame (pure pursuit) and hits within 1 u (assumed; simulated). Jump chains re-launch the frame you land.
